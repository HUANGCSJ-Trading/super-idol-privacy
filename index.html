你的影像，由你掌握。

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 2 日 · 適用 App：Super Idol（com.huangcsj.superidol）

本政策說明 Super Idol 遊戲開發者（下稱「我們」）如何處理你使用遊戲時的資料，包含 TestFlight 測試版。廣告及買斷章節適用於包含該功能的版本；未提供該功能的版本不會因此執行這些處理。

相機影像與姿勢比對在 iPhone 上處理。挑戰紀錄與影片儲存在裝置內，只有你主動選擇時才會存到相簿或分享給其他 App。廣告與 Apple 購買服務則可能需要網路及相關資料。

1. 相機、姿勢與挑戰紀錄

你授權相機後，遊戲使用影像辨識肢體位置、比對姿勢、計分，並錄製挑戰影片。紀錄可包含你輸入的舞台名稱、日期、分數、動作、肢體比對結果、原始影片、合成影片及縮圖。這些內容存放於 App 的本機空間；我們不將它們傳送到自己的伺服器，也不將其提供給廣告服務。

姿勢辨識不是人臉身分辨識；我們不建立用於確認身分的生物辨識資料庫。拒絕相機權限會使拍攝挑戰無法使用，你可在 iPhone「設定」調整權限。

支援麥克風錄音的版本中，首頁提供錄音開關。只有開啟開關並允許 iPhone 麥克風權限後，App 才將人聲與周圍聲音錄入挑戰影片。音訊和影片一樣保存在裝置，不會自動上傳至我們的伺服器或提供給廣告服務；你主動分享影片時，音訊也會包含在內。你可以關閉開關，或在 iPhone「設定」撤回權限；關閉麥克風錄音仍可遊玩。較早且未支援麥克風的版本不會錄製現場聲音。

臉部資料：收集、用途、分享、保存與刪除

相機影像、遊戲錄影及縮圖可能包含你的臉。Super Idol 使用 Apple Vision 人體姿勢偵測，在裝置上讀取身體關節與鼻子位置；鼻子僅作為頭部位置參考，用於姿勢比對、顯示遊戲回饋與計分。App 不做人臉身分辨識、不建立身分辨識範本，也不使用 TrueDepth 臉部網格。這些資料不被我們用於廣告、個人剖析或模型訓練。

即時關節及鼻子座標在記憶體處理，不另存為臉部特徵檔案。開始挑戰後，可能含臉部影像的原始錄影會保存在 App 本機儲存空間，即使你未按「儲存」或「分享」也會保留。遊戲紀錄另保存分數及播放疊圖所需的姿勢比對數值；按儲存或分享才產生匯出影片。原始影片、匯出影片、縮圖與紀錄沒有自動到期刪除期限，保留至 App 資料被刪除。

我們不接收這些臉部影像、錄影或姿勢資料，不將其上傳到開發者伺服器，也不提供給 AdMob、廣告商、資料仲介或受委託的第三方臉部資料處理者。App 沒有臉部資料的第三方分析服務。只有你主動選擇存到照片或透過 iOS 分享面板匯出時，可能含臉部影像的影片才會交給你選擇的目的地；iCloud 照片及裝置備份也可能依你的設定同步資料。這些由你選擇的服務或接收者不受我們控制，我們無法保證其保護措施與本政策相同；分享前請確認其政策，若不接受，請勿匯出或分享。刪除 App 不會刪除這些外部副本。

撤回同意：停止挑戰並離開相機畫面，可停止該次拍攝與姿勢處理。要撤回未來收集與使用相機／臉部資料的同意，請到 iOS「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 相機」關閉 Super Idol 的權限；關閉後無法開始需要相機的挑戰。麥克風權限可在同一設定區的「麥克風」撤回，也可關閉 App 的錄音選項。撤回權限不會自動刪除已保存的影片或紀錄。

刪除臉部資料：1.0.1（Build 30）尚無逐筆刪除遊戲紀錄功能。請到 iOS「設定 → 一般 → iPhone 儲存空間 → Super Idol → 刪除 App」移除 App 的本機錄影、縮圖與遊戲紀錄；「卸載 App」會保留資料。已匯出到照片的影片需另行刪除，若要立即移除，亦需清除照片的「最近刪除」項目。雲端、備份與已分享副本需在相應服務或接收端另行管理；還原備份可能還原資料。開發者未持有這些本機檔案，無法遠端讀取或刪除。

2. 存影片、分享與備份

只有在你按下「存影片到相簿」並授權時，App 才把影片加入相簿；我們不要求讀取整個相簿。使用「分享／上傳影片」時，影片會交給你選擇的 App 或服務，後續處理由該服務的隱私政策規範。Super Idol 沒有自有的雲端影片庫或公開動態牆。

iOS、iCloud 或電腦備份可能依你的設定備份 App 資料與相簿；這不同於我們自行上傳影像。請一併管理你的裝置與備份設定。

3. 廣告與隱私選擇

支援廣告的免費版每完成兩局，會在局間提供插頁廣告。Google AdMob 可能處理 IP 位址（含推估概略位置）、裝置識別資訊、廣告瀏覽與互動，以及當機和效能資訊，用於廣告提供、衡量與服務改善。非個人化廣告並不等於完全不處理資料。

App 設定為停用廣告個人化及發布商第一方識別碼，不要求精確定位權限。必要時以 Google 的同意管理訊息提供隱私選擇，並在允許請求廣告後才載入廣告。若需提供重新選擇入口，首頁會顯示「廣告隱私選項」，你可隨時更改選擇。關閉或撤回同意不影響先前已合法進行的處理。

詳見 Google 隱私權政策、Google 在合作夥伴網站及 App 使用資料的方式及AdMob iOS 資料說明。

4. 一次買斷與恢復購買

「永久移除廣告」由 Apple App Store 處理付款，為非消耗型一次買斷。App 透過 StoreKit 驗證交易與購買權益，並在裝置保存權益狀態；我們不取得你的完整信用卡號。確認已買斷後，App 不再請求遊戲廣告，但購買驗證、恢復購買及 Apple 服務仍可能使用網路。退款或撤銷購買可能使無廣告權益失效。

Apple 的交易紀錄與保留方式，請參閱 App Store 與隱私權。刪除 App 不等於刪除 Apple 的交易紀錄。

5. 資料保留、刪除與你的權利

遊戲設定、廣告局數與買斷快取儲存在裝置；挑戰紀錄和影片在本機保留，直到你刪除 App 及其資料。本版尚未提供逐筆刪除紀錄功能。在 iPhone「設定 → 一般 → iPhone 儲存空間 → Super Idol」選擇「刪除 App」，可移除本機 App 資料；「卸載 App」會保留資料，不適用於此目的。請先保存你要保留的影片。

已存入相簿、備份或分享至其他服務的副本不會隨之移除，需在相應服務另行刪除。Google 與 Apple 處理的資料依各自政策保留，我們無法直接刪除其所有紀錄。依你所在地法律，你可能享有存取、更正、刪除、限制、反對處理或申訴等權利；本機影片未傳給我們，因此我們無法遠端讀取或逐筆移除。

6. 兒童、安全及跨境處理

Super Idol 為一般使用者設計，並非特別針對兒童提供的服務。未成年人應與家長或監護人共同檢視本政策。若你認為兒童曾向我們提供不適當的個人資料，請聯絡我們處理。

請保護你的裝置、備份及分享目的地。我們不保證任何儲存或傳輸方式絕對安全。Google、Apple 及你選擇的分享服務可能在你的居住地以外處理資料，其保護措施依各服務政策及適用法律規範。

7. 聯絡我們與政策更新

如對 Super Idol 的個人資料處理或本政策有疑問，請聯絡 Super Idol 開發團隊：haat.originals@gmail.com。請說明你的請求與使用的 App 版本；不必寄送完整挑戰影片、密碼或付款資料。我們會使用來信地址與內容回覆、處理請求，並僅在處理與必要留存期間保留相關往來。

當功能或資料處理方式改變，我們會更新本頁日期與內容；必要時另行提示或取得同意。本政策不代表所有測試中的功能已正式提供。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · Super Idol for iPhone

This policy describes how the Super Idol developer (“we”) handles information, including in TestFlight. Advertising and purchase sections apply only to versions that include those features.

Camera, poses and recordings

With camera permission, Super Idol processes images on your iPhone to compare body poses, calculate scores and record challenges. Stage names, dates, scores, pose results, raw and exported videos and thumbnails are stored in the app’s local storage. We do not upload these to our servers or provide them to advertising services. Pose matching is not facial identity recognition, and we do not build an identity-verification biometric database. Camera permission can be changed in iPhone Settings.

Versions supporting microphone recording offer a home-screen switch. Only with that switch enabled and iPhone microphone permission granted does the app record voices and room sounds into challenge videos. Audio stays on the device with the videos and is not automatically uploaded to our servers or provided to advertising services. Audio is included when you choose to share a video. You can turn off the switch or revoke permission in iPhone Settings; you can still play with microphone recording turned off. Earlier versions without this feature do not record room audio.

Face data: collection, use, sharing, retention and deletion

Camera images, gameplay recordings and thumbnails may contain your visible face. Super Idol uses Apple Vision human body pose detection on your device to read body joint positions and a nose position. The nose is only a head-position reference for pose comparison, gameplay feedback and scoring. The app does not identify people through facial recognition, create identity-verification biometric templates, or use TrueDepth face meshes. We do not use these data for advertising, profiling or model training.

Live joint and nose coordinates are processed in memory and are not saved as a separate facial profile. After a challenge starts, the original recording, which may include your face, is retained in the app's local storage even if you do not select Save or Share. Game history also stores scores and pose-match values for playback overlays. Save or Share produces an exported video. Original videos, exported videos, thumbnails and records have no automatic expiration and remain until the app data is deleted.

We do not receive these face images, recordings or pose data, upload them to developer-operated servers, or provide them to AdMob, advertisers, data brokers or contracted third-party face-data processors. The app has no third-party face-data analytics service. Only when you choose Photos export or the iOS share sheet is a video that may include your face transferred to your selected destination. iCloud Photos and device backups may also sync data according to your settings. These user-selected services or recipients are outside our control; we cannot guarantee that their protections equal this policy. Review their policies before exporting or sharing, and do not export or share if you do not accept their handling. Deleting the app does not delete those external copies.

Withdrawing consent: Stop the challenge and leave the camera screen to stop that capture and pose-processing session. To withdraw consent for future camera/face-data collection and use, open iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and turn off Super Idol. Camera-based challenges cannot start without camera permission. Microphone permission can be revoked under Microphone in the same settings area, or recording audio can be disabled in the app. Revoking permission does not automatically delete existing videos or history.

Deleting face data: Version 1.0.1 (Build 30) does not offer individual game-record deletion. Use iOS Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Super Idol > Delete App to remove local recordings, thumbnails and history; Offload App preserves data. Delete exported videos separately from Photos and, for immediate removal, its Recently Deleted collection. Manage cloud, backup and shared copies separately through their services or recipients; restoring a backup may restore data. The developer does not hold these local files and cannot remotely read or delete them.

Photos, sharing and backups

Videos are added to Photos only at your request and with permission. We do not request access to your entire photo library. Sharing transfers the selected video to the app or service you choose, whose policy then applies. We do not operate a cloud video library or public feed. iOS, iCloud or computer backups may separately retain app data and photos according to your settings.

Advertising and choices

Ad-supported versions offer an interstitial between games after two completed challenges. Google AdMob may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions, crash and performance information for advertising, measurement and service improvement. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. We disable ad personalization and publisher first-party IDs and do not request precise location permission.

Where needed, Google’s consent messages collect choices before ad requests are allowed. A home-screen advertising privacy option is shown when required so you can change your choices. Withdrawal does not invalidate prior lawful processing. See Google Privacy Policy and Google’s partner-services data policy.

Purchases

Apple processes the non-consumable, one-time Remove Ads purchase. StoreKit verifies transactions and entitlements; the app caches entitlement status locally. We do not receive full payment-card details. Once ownership is confirmed, the app stops requesting game ads. Verification and restoration may still use the network. Refunds or revocation can remove the entitlement. Apple retains transaction information under its App Store privacy policy.

Retention, deletion and rights

Settings, ad-cycle counts, purchase status, records and videos remain locally until app data is deleted. Individual record deletion is not currently provided. Use Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Super Idol → Delete App to remove local app data, after saving videos you want to keep. Offload App preserves data. Copies in Photos, backups or other services require separate deletion. Deleting the app does not delete Apple purchase records or all data held by Google.

Depending on applicable law, you may have access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or complaint rights. We cannot remotely access or delete local videos that we never received. Providers retain data under their own policies. Protect your device and backups; no storage or transmission is absolutely secure.

Children, international processing and contact

The app is designed for a general audience, not specifically for children. Minors should review this policy with a parent or guardian. Contact us with concerns about children’s information. Apple, Google and chosen sharing services may process data outside your country under their own policies and applicable law.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Super Idol development team at haat.originals@gmail.com. Describe your request and app version; do not send full challenge videos, passwords or payment details. We use your email address and message to respond and handle your request, retaining correspondence for that purpose and any necessary retention period.

We will update this page when practices change, and provide additional notice or request consent where necessary. This policy does not mean every feature in development is already available.
