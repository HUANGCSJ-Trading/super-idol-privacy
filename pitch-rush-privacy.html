相機與動作

Pitch Rush 以 iPhone 前鏡頭辨識肩膀、手肘與手腕，將空手投球動作換算成遊戲模擬球速，並非實際球速測量。相機影像與關節座標僅在裝置記憶體即時處理；不錄影、不建立臉部模型、不保存影像或關節座標，也不傳給開發者、Google 或廣告商。

返回首頁或遊戲得出成績時即停止鏡頭。你可在 iOS 設定的相機權限中停用 Pitch Rush；停用後無法進行鏡頭遊戲。

最高模擬球速、最近 30 次的模擬球速／投球手／時間及設定保存在手機。刪除 App（非卸載 App）可移除這些本機資料；裝置備份可能保留或還原資料。只有你選擇分享時，成績圖片才會交給所選 App 或服務。分享圖片不包含相機照片。外部副本須由你在接收服務中管理。

Camera and motion

Pitch Rush uses the front camera to detect shoulders, elbows and wrists and convert an empty-handed throw into a game speed estimate, not real ball velocity. Frames and joint coordinates are processed only in device memory. They are not recorded, saved, used to build face profiles or sent to the developer, Google or advertisers. The camera stops when a result is produced or you leave the game. Revoke camera access in iOS Settings to prevent future capture; camera gameplay will then be unavailable.

Records and sharing

Your best simulated speed, the most recent 30 speeds, throwing hand, timestamps and settings stay on the device. Delete App, rather than Offload App, removes local data. Device backups may retain or restore them. Sharing sends a score image, without camera photos, only to the app or recipient you choose. Manage shared and backed-up copies separately.

Free users may choose Google AdMob ads after a result. TestFlight uses test ads. Google SDKs may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, identifiers, diagnostics, ad requests and interactions to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Camera frames and pose data are never provided to Google. We do not request IDFA tracking permission and request non-personalized ads; such ads can still use necessary storage and the data described above. Applicable regions receive consent forms, with an advertising privacy options entry in the purchase screen when required. Open that screen through the $4.99 / purchased entry on Home.

The US base price for permanently removing ads is US$4.99, a one-time non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. Apple displays local prices and handles payments; we do not receive card details.
